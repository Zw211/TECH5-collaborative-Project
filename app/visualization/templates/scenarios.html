Reproducible Benchmark Scenarios

Load pre-configured datasets designed to demonstrate the full range of mathematical matching cases, trade-offs, and edge cases required by the TECH5 specification.

1. Standard Trade-Off Benchmark

Feasible • Multi-Solution

10 students with varied technical competencies (Python, SQL, ML, Web, FinTech) and 4 projects (capacity 3 each = 12 total capacity). Demonstrates 3 distinct Pareto-optimal solutions highlighting the trade-off between student preference satisfaction and project skill fit.

2. Fixed Assignment & Rerun Scenario

Fixed Constraint

Standard cohort with student S02 locked to Project P2 (Cloud Data Pipeline) under a formal fellowship grant. Demonstrates strict preservation across all solutions and illustrates how fixing one student impacts outcomes for others.

3. Capacity Deficit (Infeasible Case)

Infeasible

Reduces project capacities such that total available places (6) is strictly less than cohort size (10). Demonstrates graceful mathematical infeasibility detection and bottleneck diagnostic reporting without crashing.

4. Contradictory Constraints (Validation Error)

Invalid Input

Contains 4 fixed assignments designated for Project P1 (which has a maximum capacity of 3). Demonstrates pre-solver validation error reporting and clear rejection of contradictory inputs.

Database Reset

Wipes all students, projects, skills, preferences, and fixed assignments from the database.
